# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Touhou music listeners who want to discover music, play it independently, and listen together with other fans through live parties.

## Product Purpose

GensokyoMusic brings Touhou music discovery, playback, library management, social features, and synchronized listening into one focused mobile experience. Success means listeners can move naturally from hearing a song to exploring its related artists, circles, and albums without losing playback context.

## Brand Personality

Modern, immersive, and energetic. The product should feel atmospheric and music-led while keeping familiar mobile controls easy to understand.

## Anti-references

Avoid a generic streaming-app clone, excessive decoration that competes with album artwork, unfamiliar interaction patterns, and disabled states that are too faint to identify.

## Design Principles

- Keep listening uninterrupted while users explore related music.
- Prefer familiar mobile interactions and clear progressive disclosure.
- Surface useful Touhou music relationships at the moment they are relevant.
- Preserve the existing deep-midnight visual identity and restrained accent usage.
- Handle unavailable or incomplete metadata honestly without blocking the rest of the player.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast where applicable. Interactive targets should remain comfortably tappable, disabled controls must stay identifiable, and motion should respect reduced-motion preferences.
